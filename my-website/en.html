Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is my data?

We never upload any audio or personal data. VocalMap strictly adheres to a 100% local privacy baseline. All AI inferences and diagnoses are computed offline entirely on your CPU/GPU.

What if the initial model download is slow?

For users in mainland China, the client is pre-configured with high-speed dedicated mirror sources for silent, automated pulling of the SOTA audio separation networks, bypassing slow overseas connections.

What GPU specs are required?

Possessing an NVIDIA dedicated GPU (>6GB VRAM recommended) automatically enables CUDA acceleration. If no dedicated GPU is found, the program gracefully degrades to multi-threaded CPU inference optimization, ensuring full functionality.
